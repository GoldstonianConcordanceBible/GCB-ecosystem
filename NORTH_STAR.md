# North Star

## Mission
Build a sustainable, ethical ecosystem that funds ministry expansion and spreads the Word through structured teaching and discipleship systems.

## Non-negotiables
- Integrity > hype
- Clarity > complexity
- Cashflow before expansion
- Measurable execution every week
- Audience trust is the asset

## 12-month target outcomes
- Email list growth is consistent weekly
- One flagship offer is live and improving monthly
- Membership recurring revenue is established
- Certification pathway is designed and piloted

## “Stop doing” list
- Starting new sub-projects before completing the weekly commitments
- Rebuilding branding weekly
- Overstuffing titles with hashtags
- Shipping without tracking results
