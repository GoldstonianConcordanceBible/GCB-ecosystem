# Operating System

## Weekly Commitments (the 4 pillars)
1) Content Batch: 5–10 Shorts + 1 longform or live
2) Funnel: 1 improvement/test per week
3) Offer: 1 deliverable shipped + 1 sales push
4) Outreach: 5–20 targeted touches weekly

## Standard weekly workflow
- Plan → Produce → Publish → Promote → Measure → Improve
