# Accountability System

## Weekly cadence (Sunday or Monday)
- Update SCOREBOARD
- Write weekly plan (top 3 outcomes)
- Publish content batch
- Improve funnel (1 test)
- Advance offer (1 deliverable)
- Do partnership outreach (minimum 5 touches)

## Daily minimum (30 minutes)
- 1 revenue task (offer or funnel)
- 1 distribution task (post/short/comment)
- 1 relationship task (outreach/follow-up)

## Rules
- If it’s not in an Issue, it doesn’t exist.
- Every week ends with a written review.
- One flagship offer gets priority over new ideas.
- Decisions go into DECISIONS.md with date + reason.

## Definition of “production-ready”
- Documented
- Repeatable
- Measurable
- Works without you improvising every week
